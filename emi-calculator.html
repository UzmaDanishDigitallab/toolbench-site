Frequently asked questions

What formula does this EMI calculator use?
EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is the principal, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the number of monthly instalments.
Does this include processing fees or insurance?
No — this calculates the base EMI from principal, rate and tenure only. Banks may add processing fees or insurance separately.
