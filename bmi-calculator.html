Frequently asked questions

What BMI range is considered normal?
A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally classified as normal weight for adults, according to standard WHO categories.
Is BMI accurate for everyone?
BMI is a general screening tool and doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition — athletes and very muscular people may show a higher BMI without excess body fat.
