Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No. The compression happens locally in your browser using the HTML canvas — your image file never leaves your device.
Will compressing reduce image quality?
A small amount, controlled by the quality slider. Lower quality means a smaller file; you can preview the result before downloading.
