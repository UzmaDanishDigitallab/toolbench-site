Frequently asked questions

Can I change the order of pages before merging?
Yes — the PDFs merge in the order you add them. Remove a file and re-add it to change its position.
Is there a file size or page limit?
No hard limit is set, but very large PDFs may take a few seconds longer since everything processes in your browser.
